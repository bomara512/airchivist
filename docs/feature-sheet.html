Personal video library — feature ledger

ViewTube

A private catalog for the YouTube videos worth keeping — saved, tagged, queued, and resurfaced on your own terms, from a browser extension down to the crawler that stocks the shelves.

10 areas cataloged · 34 features shipped · 11 queued

Library & browsing

  • Central library of saved videos — thumbnails, titles, channel, view count, duration, and date added
  • Full-text search across your library
  • Filter by channel, tag, favorites-only, unwatched-only, date-added range, and duration (short / medium / long)
  • Flat "Load more" view or grouped-by-channel paginated view
  • Clicking a video opens it on YouTube and silently logs the visit — view count and last-viewed date build themselves

Tagging & organization

  • Manual tagging — attach one or more tags to any video from its card
  • Tag groups — bundle related tags under one canonical concept
  • Tag aliases — merge near-duplicate spellings into a single canonical tag
  • AI-assisted suggestions — an LLM proposes tags for thin-metadata videos; confirm or dismiss as noise
  • Retroactive tagging — apply a newly defined tag back across your existing library
  • Auto-assign ungrouped tags into existing groups, plus a related-tags explorer

Favorites & watched state

  • ★ Favorite toggle on any video, with a favorites-only filter
  • "Watched" toggle — mark a video fully watched without opening it on YouTube
  • Both are settable from the browser extension too, not just the web app

Watch Later queue

  • Dedicated Watch Later page, separate from the main library
  • Add from the main list, the Rediscover shelf, or the extension
  • Drag-to-reorder the queue
  • From the extension, toggle a video in or out at any time — not just when first saved

Rediscover shelf

  • Surfaces a rotating pool of least-recently-viewed videos, sticky for a week
  • Mark a video watched right from the shelf — it drops out of rotation
  • Remove a video from the shelf without marking it watched, or refresh the pool on demand

Archive

  • Hide videos from the main view without deleting them
  • Dedicated Hidden page to review archived videos
  • Restore back to the library, or delete permanently

Creator / channel tracking

  • Track YouTube channels as first-class entities, separate from individual videos
  • Searchable, sortable channel listing page
  • Bookmark a channel straight from its YouTube page via the extension

Browser extension

  • One click saves the current video to a Firefox bookmark and your library
  • At save time, optionally also queue for Watch Later and/or mark a favorite in the same click
  • For videos already saved — archive, restore the browser bookmark alongside it, or toggle Watch Later on/off any time
  • Video titles on YouTube itself are color-coded as you browse — flagging what you've already saved, hidden, or track as a channel, including in the related-videos sidebar
  • Bookmark a creator's channel page directly

Quick-add without the extension

  • A draggable bookmarklet for browsers without the extension, with an on-page toast showing add progress and result

Behind the scenes: the crawler

  • Bulk-ingests existing Firefox bookmarks (JSON or HTML export) to backfill your library
  • Fetches rich metadata per video — title, description, view count, duration, thumbnail, channel — with graceful handling of deleted or private videos
